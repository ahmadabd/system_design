# E-Commerce Return & Refund Policy

## 1. Return Window & Eligibility
- **Standard Returns**: Customers may return eligible products within **30 calendar days** of the delivery date.
- **Condition Requirements**: Items must be unused, unwashed, in original packaging, with all manufacturer tags and seals intact.
- **Non-Returnable Items**: Perishable goods, personal hygiene items, intimate apparel, software licenses, gift cards, and final clearance items are non-returnable unless defective.

## 2. Return Process
1. Navigate to your Account Dashboard or request a return via Customer Support.
2. Select the order and specific items you wish to return, specifying the reason.
3. Print the prepaid return shipping label provided in your account.
4. Securely pack the item and drop it off at any authorized courier location within **7 days** of label creation.

## 3. Shipping Costs & Restocking Fees
- **Defective / Incorrect Items**: Return shipping is **100% free**, covered by our store.
- **Change of Mind / Sizing Returns**: A standard **$4.99 return shipping deduction** applies to the refund total.
- **Restocking Fee**: Large electronics, furniture, and appliances incur a **10% restocking fee** if returned open-box.

## 4. Refund Processing Time
- Once the returned package arrives at our fulfillment center, inspections take **2 to 3 business days**.
- Approved refunds are credited to the **original payment method**:
  - **Credit / Debit Cards**: 3 to 7 business days depending on your issuing bank.
  - **Store Credit / Digital Wallet**: Instant (within 1 hour) upon return approval.
  - **Cash on Delivery (COD)**: Bank transfer initiated within 5 business days after IBAN verification.
