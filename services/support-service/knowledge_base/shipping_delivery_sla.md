# Shipping & Delivery Service Level Agreement (SLA)

## 1. Fulfillment & Processing Times
- **Order Processing**: Orders placed before **2:00 PM EST (Monday–Friday)** are processed and dispatched on the same business day.
- Orders placed after 2:00 PM EST or over weekends/holidays are dispatched on the next business day.

## 2. Shipping Options & Delivery Estimates
- **Standard Shipping**:
  - Delivery time: **3 to 5 business days**.
  - Cost: Free on orders over $50; flat rate $5.99 for orders under $50.
- **Express / Next-Day Delivery**:
  - Delivery time: **1 to 2 business days**.
  - Cost: $14.99 flat rate.
- **International Shipping**:
  - Delivery time: **7 to 14 business days** depending on customs clearance.
  - Duties & Taxes are calculated and collected at checkout (DDP - Delivered Duty Paid).

## 3. Order Tracking & Statuses
Once an order is shipped, customers receive an automated SMS and email containing the tracking URL.
- **PENDING**: Order placed, waiting for payment capture and warehouse routing.
- **PAID / PROCESSING**: Payment verified, items being picked and packed at the fulfillment center.
- **SHIPPED**: Package transferred to courier partner; tracking number is active.
- **OUT_FOR_DELIVERY**: Courier agent is out on route to deliver to destination address.
- **DELIVERED**: Successfully handed over or placed at designated safe drop location.

## 4. Delivery Delays & Exceptions
- **Weather / Carrier Delays**: If a package is delayed beyond the estimated SLA by more than **48 hours**, our support team automatically triggers carrier inquiry and offers a $10 shipping credit.
- **Failed Delivery Attempts**: Couriers make up to **3 delivery attempts** before holding the package at a local depot for 5 days.
