# Damaged, Defective, or Missing Items Protocol

## 1. Reporting Damaged or Defective Items
If an item arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect:
- **Reporting Window**: Customers must report the issue within **48 hours** of package delivery.
- **Required Evidence**:
  1. High-resolution photo of the shipping box with the carrier label visible.
  2. Clear photos or a 5-second video showing the specific defect or damage.
  3. Description of whether the inner packaging was compromised.

## 2. Resolution Options for Damaged Goods
Once the claim is reviewed (within **4 business hours**):
- **Immediate Free Replacement**: An expedited replacement is dispatched with Next-Day Shipping at zero additional charge.
- **Full Refund**: If the product is out of stock or the customer prefers a refund, a 100% refund (including original shipping fees) is issued immediately.
- **Keep Item with Partial Credit**: In cases of minor cosmetic flaws, customers may opt for a **30% partial refund/store credit** and keep the item without hassle.

## 3. Missing Packages / "Marked as Delivered but Not Received"
1. Verify surrounding delivery locations (front porch, side gate, building reception, neighbors).
2. Wait 24 hours (carriers occasionally scan packages as "Delivered" shortly before physical drop-off).
3. If still missing after 24 hours, contact Support. We will initiate a Lost Package Investigation and dispatch a replacement or issue a full refund within **24 hours**.
