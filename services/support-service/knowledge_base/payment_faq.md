# Payment FAQ & Billing Troubleshooting

## 1. Supported Payment Methods
- **Credit / Debit Cards**: Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover.
- **Digital Wallets**: Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal.
- **Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL)**: Klarna, Afterpay (installments with 0% APR).
- **Cash on Delivery (COD)**: Available in select regions for orders under $250.

## 2. Common Payment Failures & Solutions
- **3D Secure / OTP Failure**: Ensure your mobile number is updated with your bank to receive SMS one-time verification passwords.
- **Insufficient Funds / Card Limits**: Contact your card issuer to raise daily online transaction limits.
- **Billing Address Mismatch**: The billing address entered during checkout must exactly match the address on file with your credit card provider.

## 3. Double Charges / Pending Authorizations
- If your order failed or was cancelled but you see a pending charge on your bank statement, this is a **temporary pre-authorization hold**, not an actual charge.
- Your bank will automatically release the held funds within **24 to 72 hours**.

## 4. Invoices & Tax Receipts
- Official VAT / GST itemized tax invoices are automatically attached as PDFs in your order confirmation email and are available for download under your Account -> Order History.
