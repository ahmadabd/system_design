# Order Cancellation & Modification Policy

## 1. Cancellation Window
- **Self-Service Cancellation**: Orders in **PENDING** or **PROCESSING** state can be cancelled within **60 minutes** of order placement directly from your user dashboard.
- **Post-60 Minute Window**: Once an order moves into warehouse packing or **SHIPPED** status, it cannot be intercepted or cancelled in flight.

## 2. Options if Order Has Already Shipped
- If the order has already shipped and you no longer want the items:
  1. **Refuse Delivery**: Politely decline the package upon courier delivery. The carrier will return it to sender at no extra cost.
  2. **Standard 30-Day Return**: Accept the delivery and create a return request in your dashboard to obtain a prepaid return label.

## 3. Order Modifications
- **Address Changes**: Allowed while in PENDING status. Once payment is captured and the order is routed to fulfillment, addresses cannot be changed due to fraud prevention protocols.
- **Item / Quantity Changes**: Orders cannot be modified once placed. To change items, cancel the existing order and place a new one.

## 4. Refund for Cancelled Orders
- Cancelled orders trigger an immediate automatic reversal.
- Funds are unlocked on your bank/card account within **1 to 3 business days**.
